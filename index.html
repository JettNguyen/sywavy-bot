SyWavy Bot

A private assistant for the artist SyWavy and his team. It posts the videos they choose, writes caption drafts, and shows them how each post did.

What it does

  • Posts what the team approves. A team member picks a video, checks its caption for each platform, and taps Post it or sets a time for it. Only then does the bot upload that video to SyWavy's YouTube channel (as a Short), Instagram or TikTok. It never posts on its own, and it never changes a caption after the team has approved it.
  • Writes caption drafts. It suggests a caption for each platform, which the team can edit or replace before posting.
  • Reads the numbers. It reads how each post did on YouTube, Instagram and TikTok straight from those platforms, so nobody has to screenshot stats.

Who it is for

It connects only to SyWavy's own accounts: YouTube @sywavy, Instagram @sywavvy and TikTok @sywavy. It is not offered to anyone else, and there is nothing to sign up for.
