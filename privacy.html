SyWavy Bot

Privacy policy

Last updated September 25, 2026

SyWavy Bot is a private tool run by Jett2Fly for the artist SyWavy. It connects to SyWavy's own accounts and no one else's. This page says what it reads, what it does with that, who else sees it, and how to remove it.

What it reads

  • YouTube, through YouTube API Services: the channel's videos with their titles, publish times, lengths, and view, like and comment counts; YouTube Analytics for those videos (views, average view duration, shares, subscribers gained); and comments on the channel's videos, with each commenter's display name.
  • Instagram: the account's posts with their captions, times and links; their insights (views, reach, likes, comments, saves, shares, average watch time); and comments, with each commenter's username.
  • TikTok: the account's display name and follower count, and its public videos with their titles, lengths, and view, like, comment and share counts.

It does not read direct messages, contacts, email, location or payment details.

What it does with it

  • Shows the team how each post did and which clip it came from.
  • Uses those numbers to plan the next posts.
  • Drafts replies to new comments for the team to approve. A reply is posted only when a team member taps Post. The bot publishes nothing else on any platform.

None of it is sold or rented, used for advertising, or used for anything beyond running SyWavy's own accounts.

Who else sees it

  • Anthropic. Post titles, numbers and comment text are sent to Anthropic's Claude AI models, which write the summaries, plans and draft replies.
  • Telegram. The bot's updates, including post numbers and the comments it drafted replies to, go to the team's private Telegram group.
  • Google Drive. The team's posting calendar, in its own Google Drive, lists post titles and YouTube views.

No one else receives it, unless the law requires it.

Where it is kept, and for how long

On the one computer the bot runs on, and in that computer's private backup. The sign-in tokens for each platform are stored there too, in files only the owner's account can read. Post numbers are kept so posts can be compared over time, and draft replies are kept as a record of what was posted or skipped. Anything is deleted on request.

Removing access

  • Google and YouTube: remove SyWavy Bot on Google's security settings page.
  • Instagram: remove it in Instagram's settings, under Apps and websites.
  • TikTok: remove it in TikTok's security settings, where the apps with access are listed.

Once access is removed, the bot can no longer read or post anything there. To have stored data deleted too, or to have a stored copy of your comment deleted, email the address below.

Google and YouTube

SyWavy Bot uses YouTube API Services. Google's own handling of data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy, and using YouTube through the bot is covered by the YouTube Terms of Service.

Changes

If any of this changes, this page changes with it and the date at the top moves.
